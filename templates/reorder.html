{# Mathematical Formulation Reference Card #}

📐 {{ t('Inventory Optimization Methodology') }}

StockSense applies a non-stationary (s, S) periodic review policy calibrated for retail operations:

1. Lead-Time Demand (LTD) LTD = μ_daily × Lead Time

Expected cumulative consumption during supplier replenishment delay.

2. Safety Stock (SS) SS = Z_α × σ_daily × √(Lead Time + Review Period)

Buffers demand uncertainty up to the chosen service level (e.g. 95% = 1.645).

3. Target Stock & Order Qty Target = LTD + (μ × Review) + SS Order = max(0, min(Target - Usable - Incoming, ShelfLifeCap))

Caps orders against perishable batch life and rounds up to vendor pack size.
